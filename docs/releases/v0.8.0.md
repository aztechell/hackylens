# HackyLens 0.8.0 — Standalone MicroPython Apps

Save a script from HackyLens Code, choose **PIN TO MENU** on the device, then
launch it from the main menu with the buttons. The
[color tracker](https://github.com/aztechell/hackylens/blob/v0.8.0/examples/color_tracker.py)
shows how to tune the color threshold, track an object and save a BMP and its
coordinates to microSD. This workflow works on HUSKYLENS SEN0305 and Sipeed
Maix Cube.

## Changes

- MicroPython can scan captured frames for QR codes and AprilTags, track
  colors, and write frames and results to `/PYDATA` on microSD.
- Cube's ACCELEROMETER has a level view, an X/Y/Z trace and an event view for
  the MSA301.
- QR-CAMERA and MicroPython share the updated QR decoder. The release also
  corrects camera pixel order in live preview and saved photos, and stabilizes
  SEN0305 camera capture at 320 × 240 and 640 × 480.
- The shared microSD archive includes all FACE DETECT, FACE RECOG and OBJECT
  DETECT models, with the CanMV license beside the recognition models.

## Downloads

| Board | Firmware | Flasher metadata |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HUSKYLENS SEN0305 | `hackylens-huskylens-sen0305-v0.8.0.bin` | `hackylens-huskylens-sen0305-v0.8.0.json` |
| Sipeed Maix Cube | `hackylens-sipeed-maix-cube-v0.8.0.bin` | `hackylens-sipeed-maix-cube-v0.8.0.json` |

Keep the two files for your board in the same directory when flashing. For
either board, copy the contents of `sdcard/` from `hackylens-sdcard-v0.8.0.zip`
to the root of a FAT32 microSD card. `SHA256SUMS.txt` verifies all six downloads.
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt` contains the firmware licenses.

## Hardware verification

- A pinned color tracker ran on both boards, wrote output to microSD and
  returned to the launcher on BACK.
- Cube read 20 of 20 on-screen QR presentations within one second in
  MicroPython and 19 of 20 in QR-CAMERA with a reboot before each trial.
  A separate test verified that QR-CAMERA resumed scanning on 20 consecutive
  opens without a reboot. The Cube hardware log also records optical AprilTag
  recognition and MSA301 motion, tap and double-tap events.
- On SEN0305, diagnostic firmware sampled about 1,000 color-bar frames at
  each of 320 × 240 and 640 × 480 at the revised camera clock and detected no
  frame corruption. The final camera fix was checked with live capture at
  both sizes and three camera starts without timeouts.
- The 0.8.0 binaries booted on both boards and ran a MicroPython check that
  captured a frame and saved a BMP and text file to microSD.

See the [hardware and test conditions](https://github.com/aztechell/hackylens/blob/v0.8.0/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#supported-hardware-and-current-qualification).
