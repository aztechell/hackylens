# HackyLens 0.9.0

This release reorganizes Settings, adds AprilTag distance measurement, and
improves the responsiveness of FILES and serial communication on the K210.

## Changes

- Settings groups controls by task. Choose an accent color, check microSD
  usage, start a pinned MicroPython script at boot, and rearrange the
  launcher with **SETTINGS → Menu Order**.
- APRILTAG adds NEAR and FAR search modes and optional distance measurement
  with board-specific focal calibration. Print the distance cards from the
  [marker examples](https://github.com/aztechell/hackylens/blob/v0.9.0/examples/markers/README.md).
- OBJECT DETECT saves the enabled subset of its 20 Pascal VOC classes.
- FILES keeps button input responsive during GIF decoding. The PC flasher
  can copy CAMERA photos to the computer. [USB photo download](https://github.com/aztechell/hackylens/blob/v0.9.0/README.md#download-camera-photos-over-usb)
- SEN0305 External Link handles I2C transfers and paces UART transmission
  to prevent truncated replies at 9600 baud. Maix Cube paces HMPY console
  output while preserving the HMPY wire format.

## Upgrade and downloads

A firmware-only update preserves the settings area, MicroPython scripts in
userfs, and files on microSD. The new firmware reads older settings records
and writes schema v10 at the next successful save. Records from schemas v1
through v9 use the default launcher order. [Settings compatibility](https://github.com/aztechell/hackylens/blob/v0.9.0/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#persistent-settings)

| Board | Firmware | Flasher metadata |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HUSKYLENS SEN0305 | `hackylens-huskylens-sen0305-v0.9.0.bin` | `hackylens-huskylens-sen0305-v0.9.0.json` |
| Sipeed Maix Cube | `hackylens-sipeed-maix-cube-v0.9.0.bin` | `hackylens-sipeed-maix-cube-v0.9.0.json` |

Keep the BIN and JSON for your board together. Copy the contents of `sdcard/`
from `hackylens-sdcard-v0.9.0.zip` to the root of a FAT32 microSD card.
`SHA256SUMS.txt` verifies the firmware, metadata, shared archive, and
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`. When reinstalling an earlier version with
`hkflash.py`, use the tool from that firmware tag.

## Hardware verification

- The 0.9.0 images booted on SEN0305 and Maix Cube. On both devices SETTINGS
  opened, CAMERA captured a 640×480 frame, and the internal script filesystem
  mounted. MicroPython RUN/STOP and binary HMPY HELLO and PING also passed.
- The SEN0305 Gravity UART fix passed 300 request/reply exchanges at each of
  9600, 115200, and 1 Mbaud, plus 20 full-size replies per speed while QR-CAMERA
  was running. The 0.9.0 image passed 30 more exchanges at each speed.
  [External Link report](https://github.com/aztechell/hackylens/blob/v0.9.0/docs/EXTERNAL_LINK_PROTOCOL.md#sen0305-uart-hardware-verification)
- Development builds passed 1,000 MicroPython RUN/STOP cycles on both boards.
  Cube also passed 1,000 binary HMPY cycles after its console output was
  paced. SEN0305 passed the corresponding HMPY regression run.
  [MicroPython stress results](https://github.com/aztechell/hackylens/blob/v0.9.0/docs/MICROPYTHON_RESEARCH.md#runstop-stress-on-k210)
- Physical Grove UART/I2C exchange on Maix Cube remains pending.
  [Cube hardware coverage](https://github.com/aztechell/hackylens/blob/v0.9.0/docs/MAIX_CUBE_TEST_LOG.md#release-050-final-io-acceptance-2026-10-30)
